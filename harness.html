How It Works

No configurator.You talk, we build.

STEP 1

Tell us your setup

List the products you're running (or planning to run) — brands, lens colors, modules. Links help. That's all we need to start.

STEP 2

We spec your harness

We map every product to its own drop and send you the build sheet to approve — you see exactly what you're getting before anything is made.

STEP 3

We build, you plug in

One sealed harness arrives, labeled per product. Plug it into the factory connector, connect your parts, done.

Running a Tail-As-Turn module?

The module gets built directly into the harness, so you'll ship yours to us before your build starts — we integrate it inline and return it as part of the finished loom. Don't have one yet? We can source it for you and roll it into your invoice.
